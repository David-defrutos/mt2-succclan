# Attribution and provenance

SuccClan for Monster Train 2 is a port of [CodePointer's SuccClan for Monster Train 1](https://github.com/CodePointer/SuccClan), made with the original author's permission. The original project is licensed under MIT; see `LICENSE` for the copyright notices.

The clan concept, characters, names, card ideas, mechanics and part of the localization come from CodePointer's work. The Monster Train 2 implementation adapts those ideas to the second game's APIs and balance. Some effects require custom C# code, including Frantic and Psionic interactions; this is not an unmodified copy of the MT1 implementation.

The card and character illustrations in this release have been redrawn in a SFW style. Other UI and game assets may still derive from the MT1 project. Players who prefer CodePointer's original illustrations can follow `ORIGINAL-ART.md` to replace the corresponding PNG files locally.

Monster Train 2 loading uses [Trainworks Reloaded](https://github.com/Monster-Train-2-Modding-Group/Trainworks-Reloaded) and [Conductor](https://github.com/Monster-Train-2-Modding-Group/Conductor). Their respective licenses and credits remain with those projects.
